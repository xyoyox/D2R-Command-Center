D2R Command Center
==================

Installed version: 2.7

You can also see the live version inside the app:
the sidebar footer shows "UI v2.7" and "Bridge v2.7".
If those do not match, run Update-D2R-Command-Center.bat.

What's new in 2.7
-----------------
- REVERSED: on "Launch & Presets", Batch Launch is back on top and
  Presets sits under it, then Batch Load Order. (This simply undoes the
  swap from 2.6 - whichever order you like better, it is one line to
  change.)
- ADDED: a "Show emails / Hide emails" button on the Accounts page.
  Account emails are MASKED by default (j*******@g****.com) so you can
  never accidentally flash a real email address on stream. Click the
  button to reveal every email, or click the little eye on a single row
  to reveal just that one. The setting is remembered between sessions.
- Everything from 2.6 is still here (sidebar live status position,
  full-width credit pill, uniform Configuration cards).

What's new in 2.6
-----------------
- MOVED: the Terror Zone and DClone live status now sit directly under
  the "Command Log" button in the left sidebar, instead of being pushed
  to the bottom of the sidebar. Their drop-downs open downward.
- CHANGED: the "Rp_Casanova - YOYO Crescendo" credit is now a full-width
  pill that spans the sidebar, with a larger font that scales to fit.
  Its socials/tip drop-down matches the pill width.
- REORDERED: on "Launch & Presets", Presets now comes first, followed by
  Batch Launch and then Batch Load Order.
- FIXED: in Configuration, the Playtime Tracking and Terror Zones cards
  no longer leave big uneven gaps. The cards now pack tightly into two
  balanced columns, so every card lines up uniformly.

Folder layout (2.7)
-------------------
  app\        the actual program (server.js, app.js, index.html, ...)
  scripts\    helper scripts (PowerShell + VBS) the .bat files call
  docs\       technical README.md
  assets\     icons (d2r.ico, d2r.png)
  data\       bundled reference data (levels.txt) + sample files
  *.bat       the five launchers above
